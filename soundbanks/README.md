# Sound banks

Banks of sounds (`.vcsbnk`) go in `banks/`. Instruments (`.vcssnd`), and the banks of
instruments and drums (the drum kits), go in `instruments/`. Files are as saved by the
export icons on the Sound tab (`.json` files work too). The Sound Banks screen on the
Project tab lists everything below this folder (folders two deep are read) and imports it
into the open project; the filter there sorts by what a file holds: sound banks, sounds,
instruments or percussion.
